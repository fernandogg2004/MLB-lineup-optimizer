## Re-verification at a clean commit

`scripts/check_provenance.py`, run on a clean checkout of `07d570c`, recomputes 25 randomly sampled
team-games of the E3/E4b table (`work/games_*.parquet`) and the reference game's point estimates
from raw data with the current code. Result (`outputs/provenance_check.json`):

- maximum absolute deviation over the 25 team-games (5 quantities each): **0.0**
- maximum absolute deviation of the reference game's E[R] (used and top order): **0.0**

The paper therefore cites `07d570c` as its software version. Monte Carlo-free quantities are
reproduced bit-exactly; posterior-draw summaries depend on the recorded seeds (E4c seed 3, E5 seed 2026).
